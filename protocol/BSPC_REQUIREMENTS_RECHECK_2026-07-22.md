## 1. Verification boundary

The current BSPC [Guide for Authors](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/biomedical-signal-processing-and-control/publish/guide-for-authors) is the controlling source for journal-specific submission rules. On 2026-07-22, repeated direct web and read-only HTTP requests to that page returned a ScienceDirect 403/content-delivery error. The guide link itself is present on the accessible official BSPC [aims-and-scope page](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/biomedical-signal-processing-and-control/about/aims-and-scope), but its body could not be retrieved in this environment.

Consequently:

- requirements verifiable on accessible current official Elsevier pages are reported as **verified**;
- BSPC-specific limits that reside only in the inaccessible guide are reported as **inaccessible / conditional**, even if an older project note asserted a value;
- observations from a current BSPC article are explicitly labelled **inference from published practice**, not journal instructions;
- the previous local claim that the paper is fully BSPC-compliant should not be treated as proved until the live guide and submission form are checked at upload time.

Status labels used below:

- **PASS** — current package evidence meets a directly verified official rule.
- **CONDITIONAL PASS** — package is prepared plausibly, but the controlling BSPC-specific text was inaccessible.
- **GAP** — a verified requirement is not yet satisfied or a required author decision is missing.
- **INFERENCE** — supported by current BSPC published practice but not proved to be a submission requirement.

## 2. Executive verdict

The study has a credible BSPC scope fit. Current official BSPC descriptions explicitly include biomedical-signal modelling, machine/deep learning, wearable and remote monitoring, physiological modelling, robustness analysis, personalization, and benchmark/dataset studies. The manuscript addresses recorded exercise heart-rate signals, personalized forecasting, uncertainty, robustness to user/sport/platform shift, and frozen external validation.

The package is **not yet ready for upload**, despite passing its internal automated package validator. Three substantive blockers remain:

1. author names, affiliations, corresponding-author details, ethics determination, funding, competing interests, and CRediT roles are placeholders;
2. the code/derived-data release has no persistent repository URL or DOI, and the exact current BSPC research-data option could not be reverified;
3. the AI disclosure does not yet satisfy the current June 2026 Elsevier policy for AI-assisted code, figures, and declaration placement.

Two additional packaging issues should be resolved: if a graphical abstract is submitted, its provenance must be reconciled with the current AI/tool policy; and highlights meet the content limits but are only in `.txt`, whereas Elsevier's current standard instructions request a Word document.
